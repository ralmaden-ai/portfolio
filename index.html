What I Do

Services Built for Growth

What I build for clinics, trades and service businesses, in plain terms first and the tools behind it second.

01

Workflow Automation

The repetitive admin that eats your team's week, done automatically and logged. A form submission, an invoice or a new lead moves through checking, routing and follow-up without anyone touching it, and every step is recorded so you can see exactly what happened and when. Built in n8n, Make or Zapier, whichever fits the tools you already use.

n8n Make.com Zapier GHL
02

AI Voice Agents

A phone that gets answered, even when everyone is busy or it is after hours. The assistant takes the call, works out what the caller needs, and finishes the job while they are on the line: booked into the calendar, routed to the right person, or logged as a callback someone owns. Every call leaves a record you can read back. Built on VAPI with the decision logic in n8n, so the rules can be read, tested and changed.

VAPI Voice AI n8n Call Routing
03

CRM & Lead Systems

Every lead sorted, followed up and visible, without anyone maintaining a spreadsheet. New enquiries land in the right pipeline the moment they arrive, follow-ups and appointment reminders go out on their own, and you can see where every contact stands. Set up inside GoHighLevel: custom pipelines, automated sequences, appointment lifecycles and lead tracking.

GoHighLevel CRM Pipelines Automation
04

AI-Powered Solutions

AI where a task needs reading and judgement rather than fixed rules: scoring a lead, sorting replies, summarising research, drafting a document. I am deliberate about where it sits. AI does the reading and drafting, and anything consequential still goes to a person for the final call. Groq, GPT, Claude, Gemini and RAG pipelines, chosen per job.

Groq AI RAG GPT Claude OpenRouter Lovable Gemini + More
05

GHL Website & Funnel Design

A landing page that turns a visitor into a booked call, wired straight into the system behind it. Clean design, a clear next step, and forms that feed your pipelines and follow-ups directly, so it is one system from first click to booked call rather than a website and a CRM that happen to share a logo. Designed and built in GoHighLevel.
